# Additional Metadata

## Clarified Semantics

- **`academic_terms` vs `academic_terms_all`**: both are term dimensions keyed by `TERM_CODE`/`ACADEMIC_TERMS_KEY`, but `academic_terms` (144 rows, ~2005–2030) is the purpose-built dimension with full attributes (`IS_REGULAR_TERM`, `TERM_STATUS`, class/exam dates) and exactly one current term; `academic_terms_all` (300 rows, back to 1987) is the wider, sparser superset (no `TERM_STATUS`/`IS_REGULAR_TERM`, fewer populated class dates). Prefer `academic_terms` for current-era, semantically-flagged analysis; use `academic_terms_all` only for long historical spans.
- **`academic_term_parameter`** (3 rows) is not a term dimension but an SIS parameter map fixing which `TERM_CODE`s correspond to current/previous/upcoming term indicators (`C/P/F`) with their date range and `IS_CURRENT_TERM`. Use it to resolve "the current term" at a point in time, joined to `academic_terms.TERM_CODE`.
- **`cis_course_catalog`, `course_catalog_subject_offered`, `drupal_course_catalog`** are three overlapping course-catalog snapshots of the same institution:
  - `cis_course_catalog` is subject-level catalog metadata (one row per subject/year) with no section/schedule; several catalog attributes (`HASS_ATTRIBUTE`, `GLOBAL_REGIONS`, `MASTER_SUBJECT_ID`, `TERM_DURATION`) are entirely NULL here.
  - `course_catalog_subject_offered` is the richest grain: subject offerings per term with `SECTION_ID`, section-type flags, `MEET_TIME`/`MEET_PLACE`, responsible faculty (MIT_ID), `MASTER_SUBJECT_ID`, HASS/GIR attributes populated.
  - `drupal_course_catalog` is a narrow subset (roughly depts 1–9, ~687 subjects) lacking faculty MIT_ID/responsible-faculty coverage and has `SO_*` columns linking back to the offering/grouping tables.
  Prefer `course_catalog_subject_offered` when section, faculty, or schedule detail is needed; `cis_course_catalog` for subject-level catalog rules (GIR/HASS/grading/units); `drupal_course_catalog` only when the Drupal source lineage or `SO_` linkage is required.
- **`subject_offered`, `subject_offered_summary`, `subject_summary`** form the actual-offering/enrollment hierarchy (catalog tables above are descriptive, these are operational):
  - `subject_offered` = subject/section offering per term (one row per `SUBJECT_KEY`, `SECTION_ID="0"` here); carries cluster/units, offer dept/school, HGN. In this sample it is almost entirely Harvard cross-enrollment (HAA/NIH) with zero recorded enrollments.
  - `subject_offered_summary` = one detail row per grouped offering per term (`SUBJECT_OFFERED_SUMMARY_KEY`), rolling up sections and carrying units + cluster/subject enrollment counts across all offer sources (incl. Wellesley/Harvard cross-enroll).
  - `subject_summary` = the enrollment fact table per subject per term (`SUBJECT_SUMMARY_KEY`) with the richest registration fields: first-week/fifth-week/credit/listen counts at both subject and cluster grain, plus `SUBJECT_OR_CLUSTER`, dept, and school. For real headcounts use `subject_summary`; `subject_offered`'s enrollment columns are unreliable (zero). These three link on `SUBJECT_OFFERED_SUMMARY_KEY` / `SUBJECT_SUMMARY_KEY`.
- **`fac_*` vs `fclt_*`** building/organization/room tables: parallel versions of the same facility inventory. `fclt_building`(242), `fclt_floor`, `fclt_rooms`, `fclt_organization` mirror `fac_building`, `fac_floor`, `fac_rooms`, `fac_organization` row-for-row but add `DLC_KEY`/`HR_ORG_UNIT_ID`/HR-dept linkage and have dedicated `_hist` tables (`fclt_building_hist`, `fclt_building_hist_1`, `fclt_floor_hist`, `fclt_rooms_hist`, `fclt_organization_hist`) keyed by `FISCAL_PERIOD`. Use `fac_*` as the current snapshot; use `fclt_*` when the facilities org must be tied to DLC/HR units or when you need point-in-time history via `FISCAL_PERIOD`.
- **`buildings` (211) vs `fac_building`/`fclt_building` (242)**: `buildings` is a lean building registry (key, number, name, street/mailing address, gross/assignable square footage) for 211 buildings; the facility tables carry 242 records with richer physical/administrative attributes (parent building, site, campus sector, occupancy class, cost center, geocodes, dates). They share `BUILDING_NUMBER`, but coverage differs (211 vs 242), so a join is not 1:1 and rows may be unmatched on either side.
- **`fac_major_use` / `fclt_major_use`** are small reference dimensions (14 codes) decoding room `MAJOR_USE`/`MAJOR_USE_KEY` to `DESCRIPTION` and assignable flag; identical content, one `fac_` and one `fclt_` copy.
- **`employee_directory`, `drupal_employee_directory`, `warehouse_users`, `mit_student_directory`** are person directories all keyed by `MIT_ID` but with different granularity/population:
  - `employee_directory` = full employee name/directory detail (KRB name, preferred names, dept number/name, office).
  - `drupal_employee_directory` = employee focus on employment attributes (`EMPLOYEE_GROUP`, `EMPLOYEE_TYPE`, appointment flags) and `HR_ORG_UNIT_ID`/title.
  - `warehouse_users` = mixed population (`TYPE` EMPLOYEE vs STUDENT) with `UNIT_ID`/`UNIT_NAME` and `APPOINTMENT_TYPE`.
  - `mit_student_directory` = students only (department, `STUDENT_YEAR`).
  Choose by population (student vs employee) and by whether you need name/preferred-name vs HR-org vs unit linkage.
- **`hr_org_unit` vs `hr_org_unit_new`**: current vs new naming/reference of the same HR org unit tree (641 vs 691 units); `_new` carries `HR_DEPARTMENT_ABBR`. Both expose `HR_ORG_UNIT_ID` and DLC linkage.
- **Org/dept hierarchy sources**: `master_dept_hierarchy` (310 DLC rows) is the standard DLC-level hierarchy (levels 1–5) keyed by `DLC_KEY`/`DLC_CODE`; `master_dept_dcode_parent` (340 rows) is a separate `D_CODE` parent/child adjacency (`DEPT_ID`/`PARENT_ID`); `master_dept_hierarchy_links` is the bridge table mapping each `DLC_KEY` to many `LINKED_OBJECT_CODE`s of various `LINK_TYPE_CODE` (funds centers, profit centers, org units, SIS units). Do not conflate levels-1–5 hierarchy (hierarchy table) with the adjacency map (dcode_parent) or with link membership (links table).
- **`cip` vs `cip_with_version`**: same IPEDS/CIP taxonomy; `cip` keeps one row per `PROGRAM_CODE` for its sampled version (3059 rows), while `cip_with_version` materializes a distinct key `CIP_WITH_VERSION_KEY` per code+`VERSION` combination (6350 rows) so a code can appear under 1990/2000/2010/2020 editions. Prefer `cip_with_version` when program semantics have shifted across CIP editions; use `cip` for a single, latest-taxonomy lookup. `sis_course_description.CIP_PROGRAM_CODE` links to `PROGRAM_CODE`.
- **`space_detail`, `space_floor`, `space_unit`, `space_unit2`, `space_usage`, `zpm_rooms_load`** vs `fac_rooms`/`fclt_rooms`: the `space_*`/`zpm_*` tables form the space-management/zpm model (room-level `ROOM_SQUARE_FOOTAGE`, `BUILDING_COMPONENT`/`BUILDING_KEY`, `SPACE_UNIT_KEY`↔`SPACE_UNIT_CODE`, `HR_ORG_UNIT_ID`, `ROOM_COUNTER`), while `fac_rooms`/`fclt_rooms` are the facilities room inventory with `MAJOR_USE`, organization, area and access level. `zpm_rooms_load` carries `SPACE_USAGE`, `FLOOR` (numeric) and links rooms to space/HR org units; it is a load table, not a full reference, and joins on `BUILDING_ROOM`/`BUILDING_COMPONENT`.
- **Library/TIP course-offer tables** overlap structurally with `subject_offered` but are separate sources: `library_subject_offered` (reserve-based offering with real `NUM_ENROLLED_STUDENTS`), `tip_subject_offered` + `tip_detail` + `tip_material` + `tip_material_status` (textbook/course-material program; `TIP_SUBJECT_OFFERED_KEY`→`TIP_MATERIAL_KEY` with per-material status), and `library_reserve_*`/`library_material_status`. They are distinct lineages keyed by their own `TERM_CODE`/`SUBJECT_ID` but should not be assumed to double the coverage of `subject_offered`.
- **`time_day` (day), `time_month` (640), `time_quarter` (144)** are fiscal/calendar time dimensions that also carry `ACADEMIC_YEAR`/`ACADEMIC_TERM(CODE)` and financial-aid year; use `time_month`/`time_day` to translate `FISCAL_PERIOD`↔`CALENDAR_PERIOD`↔academic term, and `time_quarter` for fiscal-quarter flags (current/past/future). `fclt_*_hist` `FISCAL_PERIOD` casts align with `time_month.FISCAL_PERIOD`.
- **`subject_attribute`** (104 rows) is a small `SUBJECT_ATTRIBUTE_TYPE` reference for catalog subject-attribute codes (e.g. GIR/HASS types), used to decode attribute code columns on the catalog/offering tables.

## Potential Join Strategies

- **Course offer → term date/status**: join `course_catalog_subject_offered.TERM_CODE`/`EFFECTIVE_TERM_CODE`, `subject_offered.TERM_CODE`, `subject_summary.TERM_CODE`, `library_subject_offered.TERM_CODE`, `tip_subject_offered.TERM_CODE` → `academic_terms.TERM_CODE` to attach start/end dates, `ACADEMIC_YEAR`, and `TERM_STATUS`. Caveat: `EFFECTIVE_TERM_CODE` is the catalog effective term, not the offering term — filter on `TERM_CODE` for the term actually offered. For the canonical "current term," join `academic_terms.TERM_CODE` to `academic_term_parameter.TERM_CODE` where `IS_CURRENT_TERM='Y'`.
- **Offering↔enrollment roll-ups**: `subject_offered.SUBJECT_OFFERED_SUMMARY_KEY` → `subject_offered_summary.SUBJECT_OFFERED_SUMMARY_KEY`, and both → `subject_summary` via `SUBJECT_SUMMARY_KEY`/`SUBJECT_OFFERED_SUMMARY_KEY`, to pull detail rows up to summed enrollment. `subject_offered_summary`/`subject_summary` span many offer sources (Harvard `NIH`, Wellesley `NIW` cross-enroll), so restrict `OFFER_SCHOOL_NAME`/`OFFER_DEPT_CODE` when a metric must be MIT-only; otherwise cross-enrollment inflates counts.
- **Offer → grouping (dept/school)**: join `subject_offered.SUBJECT_GROUPING_KEY` = `subject_offered_summary.SUBJECT_GROUPING_KEY` = `subject_summary.SUBJECT_GROUP_ID` → `subject_grouping.SUBJECT_GROUPING_KEY` (with `TERM_CODE`) to resolve `DEPARTMENT_CODE`/`DEPARTMENT_NAME`/`SCHOOL_NAME` when the offering row lacks them. Caveat: `subject_grouping` is per term and its `SUBJECT_GROUPING_KEY` is a GUID; ensure both sides are filtered to the same `TERM_CODE` to avoid GUID reuse across terms. Disambiguation: the group key is in `subject_offered` (named `SUBJECT_GROUPING_KEY`) but in `subject_summary`/`subject_enrollable` it is named `SUBJECT_GROUP_ID` — same logical key.
- **Responsible faculty / instructor → person**: on course/offering and IAP tables, `RESPONSIBLE_FACULTY_MIT_ID` (and IAP `IAP_SUBJECT_PERSON_KEY`) joins to `employee_directory.MIT_ID` (and `warehouse_users.MIT_ID`, `hr_faculty_roster.MIT_ID`) for name/office/HR-unit enrichment; `employee_directory.KRB_NAME` and `EMAIL_ADDRESS` are the joinable business keys. Caveat: a large share of `RESPONSIBLE_FACULTY_MIT_ID` is NULL on catalog rows, and `drupal_employee_directory` stores HR/unit attributes rather than the preferred-name fields of `employee_directory`.
- **Catalog dept → department/grouping**: `cis_course_catalog.DEPARTMENT_CODE`/`SUBJECT_CODE` and `course_catalog_subject_offered`/`drupal_course_catalog` `DEPARTMENT_CODE` are numeric department codes that align with `subject_grouping.DEPARTMENT_CODE` (and `subject_offered_summary.OFFER_DEPT_CODE` `COURSE_NUMBER`); join on `DEPARTMENT_CODE`+`TERM_CODE` for consistent dept names/schools. Caveat: `COURSE_NUMBER` (offering tables) and `SUBJECT_CODE` (catalog) are the same department key but are strings like `"6"`, `"15"`, `"HST"`; `SUBJECT_ID`/`MASTER_SUBJECT_ID` should not be joined across offering tables without also matching `TERM_CODE`.
- **Building hierarchy & addresses**: `buildings.BUILDING_NUMBER` = `fac_building.BUILDING_NUMBER` = `fclt_building.BUILDING_NUMBER` links the lean registry to facilities attributes; join `fac_building.BUILDING_NUMBER` → `fac_building_address.BUILDING_KEY` (and `fclt_building_address.FCLT_BUILDING_KEY`) filtering `ADDRESS_PURPOSE='STREET'` for the primary street address. Parent/campus roll-ups use self-join `fac_building.PARENT_BUILDING_NUMBER` → `BUILDING_NUMBER`. Caveat: counts differ (211 vs 242) and many facility rows have no parent/address; `BUILDING_NUMBER` in `fac_floor` is named `BUILDING_KEY`, and in `fclt_*` the building key is `FCLT_BUILDING_KEY` — use the correct column per family to avoid NULL matches.
- **Building→floor→room**: `fac_building.BUILDING_NUMBER` = `fac_floor.BUILDING_KEY`, then `fac_floor.FLOOR_KEY` = `fac_rooms.FLOOR_KEY` (and `fclt_*` equivalents via `FCLT_BUILDING_KEY`/`FCLT_FLOOR_KEY`) to assign assignable area at floor grain down to room grain. Join `fac_rooms.MAJOR_USE_KEY` → `fac_major_use.MAJOR_USE_KEY` to decode room use/assignable flag. Space-management side: `space_detail.BUILDING_KEY` → building, `space_detail.SPACE_UNIT_KEY` → `space_unit`/`space_unit2.SPACE_UNIT_KEY`; `zpm_rooms_load.SPACE_UNIT_CODE` → `space_unit2.SPACE_UNIT_CODE` and `zpm_rooms_load.HR_ORG_UNIT_ID` → `hr_org_unit`. These are separate models — mixing `fac_rooms` and `space_detail` room counts requires matching on building component + floor + room, not a direct key.
- **Facilities org → HR/DLC and master dept**: `fac_organization.ORGANIZATION_KEY` = `fac_rooms.ORGANIZATION_KEY`; `fac_organization`/`fclt_organization` `D_CODE` → `master_dept_dcode_parent.D_CODE` (parent adjacency on `PARENT_D_CODE`); `fclt_organization.DLC_KEY` (`FCLT_ORGANIZATION_KEY` = `fclt_org_dlc_key.FCLT_ORGANIZATION_KEY`) → `master_dept_hierarchy.DLC_KEY` then `master_dept_hierarchy_links.DLC_KEY` to fan out to funds centers/profit centers/org/SIS units by `LINK_TYPE_CODE`. Caveat: `fclt_organization` has multiple rows for some `FCLT_ORGANIZATION_KEY` (up to 10 + duplicates) and `DLC_KEY` is NULL on ~8 rows; `master_dept_hierarchy_links` is many-to-many by design, so restrict `LINK_TYPE_CODE` to avoid cross-source duplication.
- **HR org unit linkage**: `drupal_employee_directory.HR_ORG_UNIT_ID`, `fclt_organization_hist/fclt_organization.HR_ORG_UNIT_ID`, `fac_organization.HR_ORG_UNIT_ID`, `zpm_rooms_load.HR_ORG_UNIT_ID` all join to `hr_org_unit.HR_ORG_UNIT_ID` (`hr_org_unit_new` for the newer tree/abbreviation). Use this to attach department names/level and `DLC_KEY` to employees, rooms, and facilities orgs. Caveat: `hr_org_unit_new` has 50 more units than `hr_org_unit`; mapping requires the `_new` rename, and `HR_ORG_UNIT_ID` is absent for a small number of employee/room rows.
- **Postal/zip enrichment**: `fac_building_address.POSTAL_CODE` (= `fclt_building_address.POSTAL_CODE`, `fclt_building_address_hist.POSTAL_CODE`) → `zip_usa.ZIP_CODE` adds `CITY_NAME`/`STATE_ABBR`/county; natively the address tables already carry city/state, and many postal rows are NULL (esp. outside MA). `ADDRESS_CITY_ID` is shared across `fac_building_address`/`fclt_building_address`/`fclt_building_address_hist` for consistent city references.
- **Point-in-time facility history**: join `fclt_building_hist` / `fclt_building_hist_1` / `fclt_floor_hist` / `fclt_rooms_hist` / `fclt_organization_hist` / `fclt_major_use_hist` on `FISCAL_PERIOD` → `time_month.FISCAL_PERIOD` to align building/org snapshots with the fiscal calendar. `fclt_building_hist` and `fclt_building_hist_1` are identical in content; pick one to avoid doubling (they share the same `FCLT_BUILDING_HIST_KEY` values across a period range). Caveat: history is snapshot-per-period, so it will produce repeated rows for a fixed building over time.
- **IAP scheduling**: `subject_iap_schedule` (MEET_PLACE/TIME/DATE) is single-term (2021JA) and `SUBJECT_ID` is NULL, so link it to a subject only via `TERM_CODE` and `IAP_DATE`/`SESSION_DATE` ↔ `iap_subject_session.SESSION_DATE` and `MEET_*`↔`SESSION_START/END_TIME`; `iap_subject_detail.IAP_SUBJECT_SESSION_KEY` → `iap_subject_session`, and `iap_subject_session.IAP_SUBJECT_SESSION_KEY` → `iap_subject_person` to reach people. Caveat: `subject_iap_schedule` is only 2021JA, so left/full joins are needed and most terms have no IAP schedule grain.
- **Time/academic cross-linking**: use `time_month`/`time_day` to reconcile fiscal and academic calendars: `time_day.ACADEMIC_TERM_CODE`/`time_month.ACADEMIC_TERM` → `academic_terms.TERM_CODE`, and `time_day.FINANCIAL_AID_YEAR`/`DEGREE_YEAR` → `academic_terms.FINANCIAL_AID_YEAR`/`DEGREE_YEAR`. Caveat: `time_day` is day-grain and sparse on academic/financial-aid years (many NULLs); `time_month` is the cleaner grain for fiscal↔academic term mapping.
- **Library reserve catalog**: `library_reserve_catalog` → `library_reserve_matrl_detail` (`LIBRARY_MATERIAL_STATUS_KEY`) → `library_material_status` to attach material status descriptions to reserve items; both carry `TERM_CODE` for term-scoped joins.

**Cardinality/filter caveat summary**: most joins rely on string keys (`TERM_CODE`, `SUBJECT_ID`, `MIT_ID`, `BUILDING_NUMBER`, `DLC_KEY`, `FISCAL_PERIOD`). Their relevant scope keys are `TERM_CODE`, `FISCAL_PERIOD`, `OFFER_SCHOOL_NAME`, and `LINK_TYPE_CODE`; NULL-heavy keys include `RESPONSIBLE_FACULTY_MIT_ID`, `MAJOR_USE`, `HASS_ATTRIBUTE`, and address postal codes.
